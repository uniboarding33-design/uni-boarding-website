About Uni Boarding

Uni Boarding – For Students, By Students

Faculty of Engineering, University of Ruhuna – 28th Batch.

Uni Boarding is a student-oriented platform intended to help undergraduates find boarding accommodation during university life.

Our Objectives

  • Help students find suitable boarding accommodation near the Faculty of Engineering, University of Ruhuna.
  • Make it easier to compare accommodation by location, monthly rent, vacancies, room type, facilities, photos and videos.
  • Connect students with landlords so they can ask about available rooms and accommodation details.
  • Give property owners a convenient way to advertise rooms, annexes and other student accommodation.
  • Make accommodation information easier to find through a simple, student-friendly website.
  • Review advertisements through an admin approval process before they appear among approved listings.

Help for Landlords

Follow these steps to create an account and advertise your boarding place.

  1. Open the registration form: Click Post a Boarding Ad, then choose Register.
  2. Enter your details: Provide your full name, phone number, email address and a password of at least 8 characters. Confirm your password.
  3. Verify your email: Open the verification email sent to you and follow its link. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive.
  4. Log in: Return to Uni Boarding and sign in using your registered email address and password.
  5. Complete your advertisement: Enter the boarding place name, location, room type, monthly rent, vacancies, contact details and description. Add photos and an optional video.
  6. Submit for review: Click Submit Advertisement. Your advertisement will remain pending until an administrator reviews it.
  7. Manage your advertisement: Use My Advertisements to view your submissions. You can edit or delete your own advertisements. Edited advertisements are sent for approval again.

Need help? Contact an administrator through the Contact Us section. Never share your password or verification code with anyone.
